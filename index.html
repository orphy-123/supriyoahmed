01 / PROFILE

Bridging research,
data & delivery.

I’m a data professional with a PhD in Machine Learning and experience spanning research, higher education and industry.

As Data Lead with the Australian Plant Phenomics Network at ANU, I work with researchers and technical teams to strengthen data governance, apply FAIR practices, curate research datasets and improve the workflows behind their collection, movement and publication.

My approach combines hands-on technical work with practical stewardship: make data reliable, document it well, and make it easier for people to use again.
